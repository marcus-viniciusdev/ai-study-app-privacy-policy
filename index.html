Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 07, 2026

Your privacy is essential to us. This Privacy Policy describes how AI Study App collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our study, quiz, and Artificial Intelligence essay correction application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Authentication Data: Name, email address, and profile picture obtained via Google Sign-In, as well as unique user ID (UID).
  • Essay Content: Typed text and images captured via camera or gallery for automated optical character recognition (OCR) and grading.
  • Academic History: Quiz results, study plans, and essay feedback.
  • Device Identifier: FCM Token used exclusively for delivering native push notifications.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is processed to enable secure login, send content for artificial intelligence analysis (OpenAI API) to generate essay diagnostics, enable social interactions with friends, and personalize your local history.

3. Storage and Security

We use cloud services provided by Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Storage) with transport-level encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Progress and history data are also stored locally in the SQLite database on your device.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

We share data exclusively with essential services required for the app's operation:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, cloud database, and file storage.
  • OpenAI API: OCR transcription and essay analysis.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Displaying advertisements.
  • Google Play Billing: Subscription management and In-App purchases.

5. Data Deletion and Rights (LGPD / GDPR)

You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all data stored in the cloud and within the app by sending an email to coffeedevstudio@gmail.com. Deletion will be completed within 7 business days.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at: coffeedevstudio@gmail.com
